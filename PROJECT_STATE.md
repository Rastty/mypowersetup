## Goal — OWNER STRATEGIC OVERRIDE 2026-11-21: CARAVAN-FIRST PRODUCT EXCELLENCE
**Primary business goal:** build the most useful trustworthy independent *caravan/campervan electrical setup buyer advisor* across several existing markets, converting real user needs into electrically compatible, locally purchase-ready affiliate recommendations and repeatable profit. **Depth in one vertical comes before breadth across verticals.**
- **Do not launch new verticals** (cabins, whole-home backup, general solar and unrelated verticals) as active expansion work. Keep existing working assets available and maintain safety/SEO, but prioritize caravan-specific complete user journeys.
- **Market rollout:** perfect CZ caravan buyer journey first, then replicate verified shared UX/calculation/catalog capabilities to SK and PL; extend to HU/RO/SI/PT based on real approved-product coverage and localization readiness, not blind copies. Do not stop existing market safety/catalog maintenance while focusing on CZ.
- **Target journey:** simple scenario-first appliance selection (nontechnical language, few questions) → robust energy/power/recharging sizing → compatible battery, solar, MPPT, inverter, DC-DC and protections when evidence permits → transparent 2–3 budget/quality choices → immediately visible exact verified local purchase offers → saved/shareable parts list and guidance. Never hide the product result behind a lead form or excessive scrolling; partial baskets must be clearly labeled.
- **Trust gates:** no technical fabrication; continuous vs peak power, voltage, cold-weather charging, safety/installation constraints and source provenance must pass. Only already-approved affiliate merchants, exact SKU, stock, localized currency, tracked destination and country delivery/checkout proof; fail closed when incomplete. Solaris excluded.
- **Readiness gate before next vertical:** genuinely usable end-to-end caravan journeys in at least **three markets**, validated compatibility/stock and production checks, measurable consented funnel and some observed commercial evidence (outbound click and/or actual approved/paid commissions). Do not invent conversion thresholds or require high revenue at zero data.
- **Working mode:** inventory existing caravan routes, calculators, UI modules and published product/catalog modules FIRST; fill the highest-impact missing buyer-journey gap without duplicating finished features. Reuse shared components through markets and hand reusable capabilities to Prometheus Core. Use world benchmarks as UX hypotheses until tested; do not treat competitors' outcomes as measured facts.
- **Priority order now:** (1) CZ caravan end-to-end gap audit and implement one user-facing scenario→recommendation improvement; (2) replicate proven end-to-end system SK/PL with strict local offer gates; (3) additional existing markets; (4) only then evaluate next vertical. Measurement and catalog validation run alongside development and are not blockers to basic usability iterations on a new site.
