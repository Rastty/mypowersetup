# RO / PT / SI progressive launch

## Goal
Move Romania, Portugal and Slovenia from private market seeds to real localized calculators without allowing unfinished markets into sitemap, hreflang or IndexNow.

## Shared release order
1. Full localized 3-step calculator on the shared deterministic engine.
2. Local search-intent research and terminology review.
3. Initial high-intent content cluster.
4. Exact-product affiliate validation for the market.
5. 390x844 mobile journey smoke.
6. Analytics parity for calculator_started, calculation_completed, product_coverage_calculated, affiliate_click, result_share_requested, result_print_requested and guide_to_calculator_click.
7. Native-language review.
8. Progressive publication: calculator and strongest decision pages first, then remaining cluster after public verification.

## Romania
- Locale: ro-RO; currency: RON.
- Initial affiliate source: ALLPOWERS International, exact-product and market-eligible destinations only.
- Priority intent: baterie autorulota, panou solar autorulota, controler MPPT, incarcator DC-DC, invertor, 12 V vs 24 V.

## Portugal
- Locale: pt-PT; currency: EUR.

- Use European Portuguese, not Brazilian Portuguese.
- Priority intent: bateria autocaravana, painel solar autocaravana, controlador MPPT, carregador DC-DC, inversor, power station vs fixed system.

## Slovenia
- Locale: sl-SI; currency: EUR.
- Initial affiliate source: ALLPOWERS International, exact-product and Slovenia-eligible destinations only.
- Keep the first public footprint deliberately lean: calculator plus the strongest 9 decision pages before expanding further.
- Priority intent: baterija avtodom, solarni/soncni panel avtodom, solarni regulator MPPT, DC-DC polnilnik, inverter/razsmernik.

## Non-negotiable rule
A market remains private/noindex until every publication gate is green. Affiliate gaps never justify unsafe or guessed product recommendations; missing coverage fails closed instead.
