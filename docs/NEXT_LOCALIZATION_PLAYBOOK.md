# Next localization playbook

This is the minimum standard for opening a new MyPowerSetup market such as Romania or Portugal.

## 1. Start private and fail-closed
- New locale starts as private/noindex.
- Do not add canonical, hreflang, sitemap, IndexNow or public navigation until the launch gate passes.
- Affiliate links remain disabled until the exact merchant/program, destination rules and market eligibility are verified.

## 2. Localize the decision model, not just the words
- Research local RV/camper terminology and search intent before publishing copy.
- Validate local electrical/safety context where it materially changes user guidance.
- Keep the shared sizing engine assumptions explicit; do not present internal thresholds as local standards.
- Add local scenarios and examples rather than translating CZ examples literally.

## 3. Preserve the proven user journey
- Three-step calculator journey.
- Explainable sizing result.
- Compatible product recommendations only after technical checks.
- Guide-to-calculator CTAs.
- Share/print result actions.
- Mobile smoke at 390x844 with no horizontal overflow.

## 4. Product catalog quality
Core categories:
- battery
- solar_panel
- controller (MPPT)
- inverter
- dc_charger
- shore_charger

Power stations are useful supplemental coverage but must not substitute for standalone MPPT/controller coverage.

Product feeds/APIs are preferred. Do not use unverified scraping to make coverage green. Tax/VAT variants do not count as distinct technical products.

## 5. SEO/publication gate
Before public launch verify:
- correct locale/lang
- canonical and reciprocal hreflang
- sitemap inclusion only for public pages
- no accidental noindex on public pages
- no private routes leaked into public sitemap/IndexNow
- analytics hard events and parity events wired
- content hub + core guides linked

## 6. Expansion versus perfection
A documented external merchant/feed gap on an existing market may remain open while a new localization is prepared, provided:
- all existing markets remain structurally safe
- preaudit is audit-ready
- mobile and language review evidence is complete
- the gap is explicitly tracked and does not represent an engine, SEO, analytics, safety or publication defect

The gap must still be closed before claiming the affected market is fully complete.

## 7. Candidate-market decision before implementation
For each candidate (e.g. RO, PT), perform a short market due diligence first:
- organic competition and SERP quality
- local search terminology and content gaps
- affiliate merchant/feed availability
- product assortment depth in the six core categories
- likely localization effort and regulatory/safety differences

Choose the market with the best combination of low competition, monetization availability and reuse of the existing platform.
