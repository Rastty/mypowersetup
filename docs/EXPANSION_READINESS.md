# Expansion readiness

MyPowerSetup uses two separate quality gates:

1. **Final market perfection gate** — used before claiming CZ/SK/PL/HU are fully complete. Existing product coverage gaps remain blockers here.
2. **Expansion readiness gate** — used to decide whether it is safe to begin preparing another private localization such as RO or PT.

The expansion gate permits only explicitly documented external/deferred gaps:
- CZ stale Reslshop feed (external 403; fail-safe catalog preserved)
- SK/PL/HU second standalone MPPT coverage while a verified additional merchant is pending

These exceptions may not hide engine, SEO, analytics, mobile, language, safety or publication defects.

A new locale must remain private/noindex until its own product, language, mobile, SEO and publication checks pass.
