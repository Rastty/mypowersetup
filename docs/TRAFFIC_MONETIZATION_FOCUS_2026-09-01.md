## Current commercial hypothesis

1. batteries,
2. MPPT controllers,
3. pure-sine inverters,
4. DC-DC chargers,
5. 230 V shore chargers.

Do not activate a merchant or product merely to fill a category. Existing fail-closed evidence, market eligibility, exact destination and electrical-fit rules remain mandatory.

## Traffic priority

Prioritize work in this order:

1. fresh high-intent search demand and pages already capable of ranking,
2. current unresolved community questions where an answer is useful without promotion,
3. component categories that materially increase purchase-ready scenario coverage,
4. conversion instrumentation that attributes calculator → guide → product → affiliate click journeys,
5. technical work only when it unlocks one of the above.
