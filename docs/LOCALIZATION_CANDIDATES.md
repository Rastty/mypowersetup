# Candidate localization decision: Romania vs Portugal

Do not implement either market from translation alone. Before choosing the next locale, score both candidates on the same evidence set:

- organic competition for camper/RV battery, solar and inverter intent
- quality and completeness of the top local content results
- local affiliate merchants and feed/API access
- coverage depth for battery, solar panel, MPPT controller, inverter, DC–DC charger and shore charger
- local terminology and search-language differences
- electrical/safety guidance differences that materially affect content
- expected effort to reach the same content + calculator + product standard as CZ/SK/PL/HU

The selected market should maximize: lower competition + viable monetization + reusable platform fit.

Implementation starts private/noindex and becomes public only after its own launch gate passes.
