## Portugal (pt-PT)
Overall terminology is coherent and strongly localized to European Portuguese. One literal engineering phrase is not natural public-facing Portuguese:
- `Falhar fechado` → prefer `Sem validação, sem recomendação`.

`tracking de afiliado` is understandable in the affiliate industry, but a human reviewer may prefer `rastreio de afiliação` depending on desired tone.

## Slovenia (sl-SI)
Core caravan/electrical terminology is understandable. Public copy should avoid untranslated internal/product language:
- `Affiliate politika` → `Politika partnerskih povezav`.
- `Affiliate in neodvisnost` → `Partnerske povezave in neodvisnost`.
- `affiliate povezave` → `partnerske povezave`.
- `affiliate omrežja` → `partnerske mreže`.
- `Fail closed` → `Brez preverjanja ni priporočila`.

A human reviewer should decide whether `inverter` or `razsmernik` is preferable as the primary term. Both are used in technical contexts; consistency matters more than replacing every occurrence automatically.

## Romania (ro-RO)
Core technical terminology matches common Romanian usage (`autorulotă`, `invertor`, `încărcător DC-DC`, `controler/regulator MPPT`). Several phrases should be polished before public release:
- `Calculul primul` → `Calculul înaintea produsului`.
- `Fail closed` → `Fără validare, fără recomandare`.
- `Ce capacitate de baterie are nevoie o autorulotă?` → `Ce capacitate trebuie să aibă bateria unei autorulote?`.
- `Dimensionează solarul` → `Dimensionează sistemul solar`.
